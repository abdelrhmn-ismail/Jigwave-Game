# Jigwave — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 18 November 2026*

Jigwave ("the app") is a jigsaw puzzle game for Android.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal
data or usage data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device
Your progress (level, coins, stars, the unfinished puzzle) and your settings
(sound, music, vibration, language) are saved **only on your own device**. They are
deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Internet and permissions
The app does not use the internet. The only Android permission it asks for is
**vibration**, used for a short buzz when a piece snaps into place (you can turn it
off in the app).

## Children
The app contains no ads, no chat, no purchases and collects no data, so it is safe
for players of all ages.
